# LEVELS L04 TO L08 (specification only, Stage 14)

**PROPOSED. Not implemented, not simulated, no solutions asserted.** Each needs the checklist in `docs/LEVELS.md` section 7 before it counts. Pass marks and numbers are left open on purpose; they come from running the reference, not from this page.

| Level | One lesson | Idea | What would make a second solution exist | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L04 | Read who you are moving | A group mostly of independents (who barely respond to a sign) plus a smaller group of commuters and followers; the sign moves only some of them | Two sockets that each catch a different profile | The sign feels useless if too many independents |
| L05 | The lock has a price | A level where one rotate is needed at a known time and a second action is blocked by the 4 s lock | Rotate first then move, or move first then rotate | Needs a spawn timing where order matters; the model has not shown one |
| L06 | A wrong sign hurts | Layout where the obvious heading sends a group into a narrow street and lowers arrivals below no sign | Not placing a sign at all, or the opposite heading | Our results so far show a wrong sign only equals no sign; no level has been found where it hurts |
| L07 | Two destinations, one bridge | Groups to School and Plaza share a narrow bridge | Staggering by placing the sign to favour one group first | Needs a third socket or a longer round |
| L08 | Choose who waits | Capacity too low for everyone; the pass mark allows sacrificing one group | Sacrifice either group | The explainer needs a claim for a chosen sacrifice |

Dependencies before any of these: a runnable engine for the Stage 07 to 13 checks, and a decision on whether to add a third socket or randomness (neither is approved).
