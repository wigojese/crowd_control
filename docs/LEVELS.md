# LEVELS L01 TO L03 (Stage 14)

Level files: `data/levels/cc_l01_r1.json`, `cc_l02_ferry.json`, `cc_l03_narrow.json`. All three use the **same map geometry** (the L01 coordinates), so the screen composition and the two sockets (129 px apart at 390 wide, 106 at 320 wide) never change between levels. They differ in street capacity, walker groups and pass mark. Checked by `python3 -I scripts/check_levels.py` (Python reference of the engine rules). The engine has not run any of them.

## 1. One lesson each

| Level | Lesson | The discoverable alternative |
|---|---|---|
| L01 School Crossing | A sign changes the route of the walkers who decide at its junction: point it at the open street. | East at North Gate or east at Canal Turn |
| L02 Ferry Landing | **Move the sign**: a new group of 10 appears at Canal Turn at 0:08, and no fixed placement can pass (best 26 of 30, goal 28). | Move at 0:05 to 0:11.5 (any time in the window), or at 0:08 for the full 30 |
| L03 Two Narrow Roads | **Balance the load**: with both routes narrow, sending everyone one way jams it. Two different placements pass: east at North Gate, or south at Canal Turn. | The south placement is not the obvious one |

## 2. Difficulty matrix

| Level | Walkers | Pass mark | No sign | Best fixed placement | Fixed placements that pass | Margin pass mark over no sign | Mid-round window that passes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| CC-L01-R1 School Crossing | 20 | 18 | 17 | 20 | AE, BE of 8 | 1 | not part of the lesson |
| CC-L02-FERRY Ferry Landing | 30 | 28 | 23 | 26 | none of 8 | 5 | move to Canal Turn, ticks 100 to 230 (5.0 to 11.5 s) |
| CC-L03-NARROW Two Narrow Roads | 40 | 29 | 27 | 29 | AE, BS of 8 | 2 | not part of the lesson |

L03 has the thinnest margin (2 walkers over the no-sign result): it is the hardest to pass by luck but also the easiest to miss by one. The pass mark rule used so far: pass mark is above the no-sign result and at most the result of the tested solutions.

## 6. Seeding and share string

- **Deterministic seeding:** the ruleset CC-RULESET-01C has **no random element**, so the level seed (1007) has no effect on any number above; two runs are identical because nothing is random, and "no unsolvable seed" is trivially true. Seeds become meaningful only if a later stage adds randomness. This is stated plainly so the seed field is not mistaken for variety.
- **Share string:** not implemented; it was not approved (the prompt says only if already approved).

## 7. Content creation checklist (for L04 onward)

1. One sentence lesson, written before the map.
2. Reuse the L01 geometry unless the lesson needs a new graph; if it does, check the map fits the free band and the sockets are at least 60 px apart at 320 wide.
3. Run no sign, all fixed placements and the candidate solutions in the reference; the pass mark sits above no sign and at or below the solutions.
4. Fewer than half of the fixed placements may pass.
5. Two distinct solutions listed and replayed; a replay file for each.
6. Run `scripts/check_levels.py` and `scripts/validate_scenarios.py`; add the level to both lists.
7. Write the first-failure copy through the explainer, and the transition sentence without claiming a result.
8. Play it in the engine and on a phone (BLOCKED so far) before calling it done.
